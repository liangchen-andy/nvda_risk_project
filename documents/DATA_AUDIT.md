# Data correction and workflow status

The portfolio workflow is now `scripts/verified_analysis.py`. Its inputs and outputs
are in `data/verified/` and `documents/verified/`. The original Pytask workflow is
retained as a legacy synthetic demonstration; it is not the source of the README's
observed-data findings.

## Findings in the legacy workflow

- Both shipped price snapshots reproduce `_build_price_data` with seeds 11 and 22,
  including simulated volumes, over 1,305 weekdays. They are not observed NVDA or
  S&P 500 prices. The generic `snapshot` provenance label obscured this distinction.
- Macro values are generated by `np.linspace`. Renaming `policy_rate` to `dgs10`
  does not turn them into the US 10-year Treasury yield.
- Daily simple returns were summed into monthly returns rather than compounded.
- Missing adjusted closes were replaced with raw closes, and the first return was
  filled with zero. Neither is appropriate for the observed-data workflow.
- Monthly rolling-beta windows of 60 and 252 were poorly suited to a 60-month sample.
- Legacy VaR thresholds were fitted and assessed on the same sample. Those counts
  do not establish out-of-sample forecasting performance.
- The overview chart averaged dollars, percentages, beta and counts. Its bars had
  no interpretable common unit. Figure failures could produce a 1×1 placeholder.
- Passing consistency gates and the previously reported coverage baseline do not
  validate these data or modeling assumptions.

## Corrections in the observed-data workflow

Yahoo chart responses for NVDA and ^GSPC and the FRED DGS10 CSV are archived with
URLs, retrieval time, units and SHA-256 hashes. Builds verify hashes and instrument
identity and require every XNYS session in the configured sample. No synthetic
fallback is available. Refreshing the bundle is an explicit operation.

Returns use vendor adjusted closes with pre-sample observations. Monthly returns
compound daily returns and are checked against adjusted month-end price ratios.
Daily beta uses a 252-session window. DGS10 retains its own units and observation
dates; alignment uses only contemporaneous or earlier yields, capped at seven days.
The yield regression is descriptive and does not claim causal identification or
real-time publication availability.

Historical and normal daily VaR forecasts use exactly 252 prior sessions. Every
forecast includes its training dates, threshold, ES estimate and realized return.
Kupiec coverage tests use only these out-of-sample dates. GARCH comparisons,
independence tests and ES backtesting are not part of the verified workflow yet.

Plots have explicit units and fail visibly on export errors. The new CI job runs
targeted regression tests and rebuilds the observed-data results independently of
the legacy document toolchain.

## Reading older artifacts

`documents/paper.md`, `documents/presentation.md`, the root PDFs,
`documents/public/` and `documents/tables/` belong to the legacy demonstration.
Their old numbers are retained for traceability, not as current observed-data
findings. Use [verified results](verified/RESULTS.md) for the portfolio.

The verified workflow uses a separate Python 3.12 environment; the legacy Pixi
environment remains Python 3.14. The original test suite and PDF builds have not
been revalidated as part of this change. No new full-repository coverage claim is made.
